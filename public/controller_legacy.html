Setup checklist

  • Connect the TV with HDMI and open /display.html on it.
  • Point the laptop camera at the players and the TV.
  • Dim the lights, then run Auto-calibrate TV.
  • Stand 2–4 m back so your whole body is in frame, then wave to join.
  • For the best perspective, stand at a measured distance and tap Calibrate FOV.
